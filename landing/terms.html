3. Description of Service

Snag AI is a browser extension that assists freelancers on the Upwork platform by providing AI-powered tools to improve their job application process. Specifically, the Service enables you to:

  • Sync publicly available information from your Upwork profile page (when you explicitly initiate the sync action) including your name, professional title, job success score, hourly rate, tier badge, earnings history, skills, bio, employment history, languages, education, and portfolio items.
  • Read live job listing data from Upwork job pages you visit, including publicly visible statistics such as proposal counts, client hiring rates, client ratings, and job requirements, to calculate a win probability and profile match score.
  • Generate personalised AI cover letters and proposal drafts using the Anthropic Claude API, based on the combination of your synced profile data and the job listing you are viewing.
  • Receive AI-generated writing tips, job signal analysis, and screening question answers displayed within the extension panel.

The Service is a drafting and analysis tool only. It does not submit applications, send messages, interact with Upwork on your behalf, or perform any automated actions. All actions on the Upwork platform are taken solely by you. You are entirely responsible for reviewing, editing, and deciding whether to use any AI-generated content before submitting it.
