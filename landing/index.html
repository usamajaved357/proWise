QUICK START

How It Works

Up and running in about 5 minutes — verify once, sync your profile, and every job page after that has a Write Proposal button.

1

Add your email and verify it

In Settings, enter the same email you use (or plan to use) on Paddle, then verify it by clicking the link sent to your inbox.

2

Add your Upwork profile URL

In Subscription, paste your Upwork profile URL — standard or custom URLs both work. Pro supports 1 profile, Agency supports 2, covering both your freelancer and agency profiles.

3

Click "Sync Profile" on your Upwork page

A circular logo button appears bottom-right on your profile page. Click Sync and it reads your rate, JSS, skills, bio, history and portfolio in a single pass — it glows green when done.

4

Add portfolio links, then write your first proposal

Give portfolio items live URLs so the AI can reference them by name and link to them. Then open any job and click Write Proposal.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Snag AI work for both freelancer and agency profiles?
Yes. Every feature — cover letter writing, job audits, profile audits and smart alerts — works the same way whether you're applying as a freelancer or through an agency profile.
Where does Snag AI actually run?
Right inside Upwork. It's a Chrome extension that adds itself to the job post and cover letter pages you already use — there's no separate app or dashboard to switch to.
Will the cover letters sound like me?
Snag AI drafts using your real portfolio, rate and tone settings, then lets you refine in place with one-click commands like "shorter" or "more confident" before you send anything.
What does a job audit actually check?
Competition level, client hire rate and spend history, and how your rate and skills stack up — then gives you a plain verdict on whether the job is worth a connect.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, all plans are month-to-month with no contract. Cancel from your billing page whenever you like and you'll keep access until the end of your current cycle.
Do unused cover letters roll over?
No, each plan's monthly allowance resets on your billing date. Upgrade anytime if you're consistently running out before the cycle ends.
